# Privacy Policy – Calculator Vault: No Ads

**Last Updated: September 19, 2026**

Calculator Vault: No Ads (“Calculator Vault”, “we”, “our”, or “the App”) is designed to help users privately store and protect personal photos and videos.

We take user privacy and data security seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes, why it is processed, and how it is protected.

## 1. Local Vault

Calculator Vault allows users to securely store photos and videos on their device.

Media imported into the vault is encrypted before being stored in the App's private storage.

The local vault can be used without creating an account or purchasing a cloud subscription.

The App uses a calculator-style interface as an access mechanism to the private vault.

## 2. Photos and Videos

The App may access photos and videos selected by the user for the purpose of importing them into the encrypted vault.

Calculator Vault does not automatically collect or upload the user's entire photo or video library.

Only media explicitly selected or otherwise intentionally submitted by the user is processed by the App.

Media stored in the local vault is encrypted.

## 3. Cloud Backup

Cloud backup is an optional feature.

Users who choose to use cloud backup may store encrypted copies of their vault media in cloud storage.

Media is encrypted before upload. Cloud backup media is stored as encrypted ciphertext.

Our cloud infrastructure is not intended to receive plaintext copies of vault photos or videos through the cloud backup process.

Cloud services may process technical information necessary to provide backup functionality, including account identifiers, encrypted objects, object sizes, backup state, storage usage, and synchronization metadata.

## 4. Google Account and Authentication

A Google account may be used for cloud-related functionality, authentication, and account recovery.

Authentication services may process information such as the user's account identifier and information required to authenticate the user.

A Google account is not required to use the local vault.

## 5. Purchases and Subscriptions

Calculator Vault may offer paid cloud storage subscriptions through Google Play.

Available storage plans may include 5 GB, 25 GB, and 100 GB plans.

Purchases and payments are processed by Google Play. We do not directly receive or store the user's payment card details.

We may process purchase-related information such as subscription status, product identifier, purchase token, entitlement status, expiration information, and storage quota to verify and provide purchased cloud services.

Sensitive purchase credentials and tokens are not intended to be publicly accessible.

## 6. Subscription Expiration and Cloud Data

If a paid cloud storage entitlement expires, new cloud backups may be disabled.

Users may be provided with a limited retention period during which existing cloud backups can still be restored or deleted.

Under the current service policy, encrypted cloud backup data may be retained for up to 30 days after the applicable cloud storage entitlement ends.

If the subscription is reactivated during the retention period, eligible cloud backup access may resume.

If the entitlement is not restored before the retention period expires, the cloud backup may be permanently deleted.

Cancellation of automatic renewal does not necessarily terminate access immediately. Access may continue until the end of the already-paid subscription period.

## 7. Firebase and Cloud Services

Calculator Vault uses Google/Firebase and Google Cloud services for functionality that may include authentication, encrypted cloud storage, backup metadata, secure backend processing, and subscription entitlement management.

These providers may process technical data necessary to operate their services according to their respective privacy and security terms.

## 8. Security

Calculator Vault uses security measures intended to protect vault data, including encryption and authenticated encryption mechanisms.

Local vault media and cloud backup media are designed to remain encrypted while stored.

No security system can guarantee absolute security, but we design the App to minimize exposure of private media and sensitive credentials.

## 9. Advertising

Calculator Vault does not display third-party advertisements.

We do not use vault photos or videos for advertising.

## 10. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal vault photos, videos, or personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate features requested by the user, such as authentication, cloud storage, subscription verification, and infrastructure services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

## 11. Data Deletion

Users can delete media from their local vault using the App.

Users using cloud backup can request deletion of their cloud backup through the App.

Cloud deletion may be processed asynchronously by backend systems to ensure encrypted objects and associated cloud records are safely removed.

Users may also request deletion of applicable account-associated cloud data by contacting us.

## 12. Children's Privacy

Calculator Vault is not specifically designed or directed toward children.

We do not knowingly design the service to collect personal information from children for advertising or profiling purposes.

## 13. Permissions

Depending on the Android version and features used, Calculator Vault may request access necessary for functionality such as selecting, importing, exporting, or removing user-selected media.

Permissions are used only for the functionality for which they are requested.

## 14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, legal requirements, or services change.

The updated version will be published at this Privacy Policy URL with a revised “Last Updated” date.
